THE HOTEL

A hotel built to keep pace with Nagpur.

Hotel West24 opened in West Nagpur, Maharashtra, with a simple premise: the Orange City doesn't sleep on a schedule, so neither should its hospitality. Every suite, every meal, every request is handled with the same unhurried attention — at 2pm or 2am.

The result is a small, deliberately quiet hotel. Twenty-four suites, not two hundred. Enough to know your name by the second night, and enough restraint to leave you alone when that's what you'd prefer.

Always open

Front desk, kitchen and concierge run on a true 24-hour clock.

24 suites only

A single small building, kept intentionally intimate.

West Nagpur address

A quiet Civil Lines block, minutes from the city's better tables.

Locally minded

Furnishings, art and pantry sourced from Vidarbha's local makers.
