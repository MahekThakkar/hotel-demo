THE HOTEL

A hotel built to keep pace with the west side.

West24 opened with a simple premise: the city doesn't sleep on a schedule, so neither should its hospitality. Every suite, every meal, every request is handled with the same unhurried attention — at 2pm or 2am.

The result is a small, deliberately quiet hotel. Twenty-four suites, not two hundred. Enough to know your name by the second night, and enough restraint to leave you alone when that's what you'd prefer.

Always open

Front desk, kitchen and concierge run on a true 24-hour clock.

Locally minded
